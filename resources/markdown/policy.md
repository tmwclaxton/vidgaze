## 1. Who is responsible for your data

**[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]** ("VidGaze", "we", "us"), the operator of vidgaze.tv, is the data controller. Contact us about privacy at [support@vidgaze.tv](mailto:support@vidgaze.tv).

## 2. What we collect

**If you just browse**

- Basic technical data your browser sends, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of the visit. This is kept in server logs for security and to fix problems.
- Anonymous viewing data, such as which videos were watched and for how long, used to rank trending videos.

**If you create an account**

- Your name, username, email address and a hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
- What you do on the site: subscriptions, playlists, likes, comments and watch history.
- If you link an account from another platform, the basic profile details and access tokens that platform shares with us, used only for the features you asked for.

**If you contact us**

- Your email address and what you tell us.

We do not ask for sensitive data, and we do not sell personal data.

## 4. Embedded videos

Videos on VidGaze are embedded from the platform that hosts them, such as YouTube or Rumble. When an embedded player loads, that platform may set its own cookies and collect data about you under its own privacy policy, for example the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for YouTube and the [Rumble Privacy Policy](https://rumble.com/s/privacy). We do not control this. Where available we use privacy enhanced embed options.

## 5. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies:

- **Essential cookies** keep you signed in, protect forms against cross site request forgery, and remember settings such as light or dark mode. The site does not work properly without them.
- **Embedded player cookies** are set by the video platforms described above when you play a video.

We do not use advertising cookies of our own. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the site may then stop working.

## 6. Who we share data with

We only share personal data with service providers who help us run VidGaze, such as hosting, email delivery and security providers, under contracts that protect it. We may also share data if the law requires it, or to protect our users or the service. Some providers may process data outside the UK; when they do, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Account data: while your account is open. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep it longer by law.
- Server logs: up to 90 days.
- Support emails: up to 2 years.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email [support@vidgaze.tv](mailto:support@vidgaze.tv) and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

## 9. Children

VidGaze is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will tell registered users by email or on the site.
